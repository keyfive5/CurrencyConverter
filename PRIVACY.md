# Privacy Policy — Currency Converter

_Last updated: July 28, 2026_

**Currency Converter does not collect any data about you.**

## What the app stores

Everything the app remembers lives only on your device:

- the currencies you have added to your list,
- the amount you last typed,
- a cached copy of the most recent exchange rates, so the app keeps working offline.

This information never leaves your phone. It is not backed up to our servers,
because there are no servers. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

## No accounts, no tracking

There is no sign-up, no login, and no user profile. The app contains no
analytics, no advertising, no tracking SDKs, and no crash-reporting service. We
do not know who you are and we cannot identify your device.

## Network requests

To show current exchange rates, the app requests a public list of rates from:

- `open.er-api.com` (exchangerate-api.com)
- `cdn.jsdelivr.net` (used only if the first source is unreachable)

These requests ask for a public price table. They contain no personal
information, no account identifier, and no description of what you were
converting — only a plain request for the current rates. As with any internet
request, the provider receives your IP address; their handling of it is
described in their own privacy policies.

## Children

The app is safe for all ages and collects nothing from anyone, including
children under 13.

## Accuracy

Exchange rates are provided for general information only. They are mid-market
reference rates and will not match the rate your bank, card issuer, or
currency exchange actually gives you. Do not rely on this app as the sole basis
for a financial decision.
